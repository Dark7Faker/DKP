# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 13, 2026**

<aside>

## About this app

DK Pace (“the App”) is developed by DarkFaker. This policy explains what information the App stores and how it is handled.

</aside>

<aside>

## Information stored by the App

The App lets you enter and save run information, including run names, scores, dates, goals, and per-level score, bonus, and death-point values. It also stores your app preferences, such as navigation and input-shortcut settings.

This information is stored in the App’s private data on your device. The App does not require an account, and the App itself does not send this information to the developer or to an App-operated server.

Run names are chosen by you and may contain information you decide to enter. Please avoid including information you do not want stored on your device.

</aside>

<aside>

## How the information is used

The App uses your entries and preferences only to provide its features: calculating and displaying run statistics, saving and reviewing runs, and applying your settings.

Dates are created and displayed using your device’s local date, time-zone, and language settings. The App does not use this information to determine your physical location or send it elsewhere.

</aside>

<aside>

## Network access and external links

The App does not include an account service, advertising, analytics, or an App-operated online service. When you choose an external link in the App, such as the user guide, GitHub, or the release page, Android opens that link in a browser. The selected website may receive information according to its own privacy policy and settings. This browser activity is handled by that website and your browser, not by an App-operated server.

</aside>

<aside>

## Android backup and device transfer

Android’s backup or device-transfer features may back up or transfer the App’s stored data, depending on your device, Android version, and backup settings. Android or the relevant backup provider controls these operations. Backed-up data may be restored to the App when you restore your device or set up another device.

</aside>

<aside>

## Data retention and deletion

Your saved runs, goal, and preferences remain on your device until you delete them or remove the App’s data. You can use **Delete Saved Data** in the App’s Settings to clear the App’s stored data. Resetting a run clears the current run’s entries; it does not delete previously saved runs.

Uninstalling the App removes its local data according to Android’s behavior. Data held in an Android backup may be restored if you later restore that backup.

</aside>

<aside>

## Children’s privacy

The App does not knowingly collect or transmit personal information from children or other users. Information entered into the App remains in its local app storage, subject to the Android backup behavior described above.

</aside>

<aside>

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the App’s data practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL, with the “Last updated” date revised accordingly.

</aside>
