# DKP

<aside>
💡

### Idea

DKP helps you track your pace in the original Donkey Kong Arcade game live during a run and analyze your performance afterward. Enter score, bonus, and deaths manually for each level and the app calculates pace and average for you. After playing, you can save your run to review it anytime.

### DK Pace Tracker

This view lets you enter level results as you play and track how you are performing now and over time.

1. **Goal:** Enter or change your target score before or during a run. A goal is optional, but some statistics compare your performance against it. If you have a specific target in mind, entering it is recommended.
2. **Level Results:** At the end of each level, enter your score. Ff the score does not already include the bonus from the level’s final board, enter that bonus separately. If the bonus was zero—or is already included in your score—leave the Bonus field blank. 
    
    If you died during the level, enter an estimated death point value, including zero if there were none. Death points affect the pace calculation for the current level, but not the calculations for past or future levels. Entering an estimated death point value can make the current score prediction more accurate, but it is optional.
    
    Tap near the left or right of the screen to decrease or increase the current level. Pace tracking starts at the end of Level 4, so do not enter data for earlier levels. Be sure to enter the Score and any Bonus for Level 4: all later calculations depend on them.
    
    You can skip entering data for other levels. This will not affect pace calculations when you resume entering results at a later level.
    
    To speed up data entry during a game, the Score, Bonus, and Death fields support shortcuts:
    
    - Score: Entering a four-digit number appends two zeros. For example, “1253” becomes “125300”. At higher levels, a five-digit number is also expanded to seven digits.
    - Bonus and Death: Entering one or two digits appends two zeros: “1” becomes “100”, “10” becomes “1000”.
3. **Level Statistics:** This panel shows calculated values that describe your current performance.
    - Pace predicts your end score based on your performance in this and previous levels.
    - Points This Level shows how many points you earned in the current level.
    - Current average shows your average points per level across all levels played in this run.
    - Next Level - Current estimates your score at the end of the next level if you maintain your current pace.
    - Needed Average shows the average points per level you need for the remaining levels to reach your goal.
    - Next Level - Needed shows the score you need at the end of the next level to stay on track for that average
    
    A field shows “- - -” when a value cannot be calculated for the current level or when required inputs are missing or incorrect. 
    
4. **Pace Over Time:** This panel shows your total death points and a chart of the pace history 
    
    The Deathpoints total shows the death points recorded across all played levels.
    
    The chart displays pace for levels where it can be calculated. Tap a chart item to select that level. Levels with a pace below your goal are shown in red; all others are green. A skull marks a level with a death entry. 
    
    Use the chart to see how your pace and average have changed throughout the run. The prediction for the most recently entered level best reflects your current performance; earlier predictions are historical. Reviewing the chart can help you learn from your run, but it is best to explore its history in more detail after playing rather than during the run. 
    
5. **Battery Save Mode:** To conserve battery during a long run without having to unlock your phone during short breaks, tap the battery icon in the top-right corner. The screen will dim and stay on. Tap anywhere on the screen to exit Battery Saver Mode.
6. **Save and Reset:** When you finish a run, save it for later review or reset the tracker.
    - Reset clears the current run’s data and returns the tracker to its starting level.
    - Save opens the save form. The end score is prefilled using the latest valid score entry and its bonus, but you can enter the actual final score if it differs. You can also give the run a name; if you leave it blank, the end score is used as the name.

### Analyze Runs

This view lists saved runs so you can review them. It also lets you open the personal-best chart.

1. **Sort Runs:** Tap the sort button in the top-right corner to sort runs by a selected property.
2. **Run Details:** Tap a run to open its details. You can review it using familiar app elements, edit its level entries to explore what-if scenarios or correct mistakes, or delete the run.  
3. **Personal-Best Chart:** Tap the chart button next to the sort button to view how your personal-best scores have improved over time. Tap a chart item to display its run below the chart, then tap that run to open its details.
